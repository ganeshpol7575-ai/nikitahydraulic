Frequently Asked

Questions from buyers.

Where is Nikita Hydraulic located?

We are located in Bhosari MIDC, Pune, Maharashtra (411026) — one of the largest industrial areas in Pune. Accessible from Pimpri-Chinchwad, Chakan, Talegaon and greater Pune.

Which rubber materials do you mould?

We mould Nitrile (NBR), EPDM, Silicone (VMQ), Viton (FKM), Natural Rubber (NR) and SBR. Custom compound development available on request.

Do you accept small MOQ orders?

Yes. We accept small MOQ orders from 10 pieces up to bulk production of 10,000+ — with the same quality attention.

Do you deliver across Maharashtra and India?

Yes. We ship pan-India with strong coverage across Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Aurangabad, Nagpur and Kolhapur.

How long does a custom order take?

Standard lead time is typically 7–15 working days depending on complexity, mould availability and quantity.
