Material Selection Guide

Standard elastomer families we process. Custom compounds available on request.

Material Durometer (Shore A) Temp Range Best For
Nitrile (NBR) 40 – 90 -40°C to 120°C Oil & fuel resistance
EPDM 40 – 90 -50°C to 150°C Weather, UV, steam
Silicone (VMQ) 20 – 80 -60°C to 250°C High temperature, food grade
Viton (FKM) 60 – 90 -20°C to 200°C Chemicals, acids
Neoprene (CR) 40 – 90 -40°C to 120°C General purpose, ozone
Natural Rubber (NR) 30 – 90 -50°C to 80°C Abrasion resistance
