Frequently Asked

Questions from Buyers in Maharashtra

Common questions about our rubber moulding services, materials and delivery.

Where is Nikita Hydraulic located in Pune?

We are located in Bhosari MIDC, Pune, Maharashtra (411026). Bhosari is one of the largest industrial areas in Pune, and we're easily accessible for clients across Pimpri-Chinchwad, Chakan, Talegaon, and greater Pune.

Which rubber materials do you mould?

We mould Nitrile (NBR), EPDM, Silicone (VMQ), Viton (FKM), Natural Rubber (NR) and SBR. Custom compound development is also available — share your application requirement and we'll recommend the right material.

Do you accept small MOQ rubber moulding orders?

Yes. We accept small MOQ orders starting from 10 pieces, right up to bulk production runs of 10,000+. Every order — regardless of size — gets the same quality attention and dimensional checks.

Do you deliver across Maharashtra and India?

Yes. We ship pan-India with a strong delivery network across Maharashtra — Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Aurangabad, Nagpur, Kolhapur — and to industrial hubs nationwide. Packaging is done to prevent transit damage.

How long does a custom rubber moulding order take?

Standard lead time for custom parts is typically 7–15 working days depending on complexity, mould availability and order quantity. Repeat orders with existing moulds ship faster. We always confirm timelines upfront.

How do I get a quote for rubber components?

The fastest way is to send us your requirement on WhatsApp at +91 81490 02309 — a drawing, sample photo, or specification. Our engineering team reviews technically and responds with material recommendation and pricing, usually within 24 hours.

Do you manufacture custom rubber parts from drawings?

Yes — this is our core strength. We manufacture fully custom rubber components from technical drawings, samples or specifications. Send your 2D drawing, 3D file or sample for review.
